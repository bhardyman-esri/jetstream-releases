Launch-crash recovery

  • Fixed the startup crash caused by SwiftPM resource-bundle lookup.
  • About's GitHub link now opens the public distribution repository.

If Jetstream crashes at launch, download this release's DMG, drag Jetstream into Applications, and choose Replace. Your preferences and configuration are preserved. Automatic updates resume after the corrected app launches.

Changed

  • Export now names each result explicitly and includes FWD HTML and Outlook HTML. Suggested filenames identify the deliverable instead of "output."
  • Clear Workspace moves out of Export and into the main toolbar, directly after Export.
  • A shorter, four-step onboarding tour focuses on choosing deliverables, adding an email, converting, and reviewing. Maps.com Beta gets a mention; appearance controls stay in Settings.
  • Release notes are tighter, with a little personality and no feature amnesia.

Fixed

  • Calcite keeps inline body links beside their linked text instead of moving their URLs to the end of the paragraph. Preference-update footer links keep their separate-line format.
  • Primary buttons, including Convert, now use a solid app-color fill so they stand out in translucent windows.
  • Maps.com emails now show the Maps.com sender name and bundled brand logo in Email Preview.
  • Worked around an Xcode 26.6 compiler crash in the Launch at Login toggle.
  • Release-note HTML now keeps wrapped bullets together and renders bold labels and code without stray Markdown.
